Tracking and ad personalisation

On iOS, the game asks for permission before using your advertising identifier. If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalised. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent form before personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in the game: Settings → Ad privacy choices.

Children

The game is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided data to us, contact us and we will delete it.

Retention and your rights

Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may ask us to access, correct or delete data about you, or object to its processing. Because the game has no accounts, we may need your device's app instance details to find your data; contact us and we will help.

Deleting the game removes all progress stored on your device.
